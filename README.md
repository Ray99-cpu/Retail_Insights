### Introduction

#### The aim of this project is to identify trends, insights, and the impact of promotional markdowns on sales. Provide comprehensive, visually appealing sales reports and insights to assist in strategic decision-making. This forms one strand of secondary market research on which the business will be more informed regarding decision making in the future.

### Resources

- PC
- Time – 2 days
- Dataset – Online Retail Dataset
- Code Sources: LMS, chatGPT, WC3 Schools

### Scope

#### The customer base is regional and so demand will be affected by the state of the domestic economy and international economy. This will give the retailer an understanding as to why products may sell depending on the markdown. Though this is not the only factor affecting sales there are other factors to consider like the following:
-	Government policy
-	Level of inflation
-	Interest rates
	Geographic location
-	Trade agreements
-	Technological advancements

### The demand for most products is elastic and can be viewed as luxuries, so as price increases less will be demanded and vice-versa.

### Aims and Objectives
### The aims are as follows:
-	To identify which store experiences the most purchases.
-	To identify customers who purchase frequently
-	To identify products that are purchased infrequently, so they will take up much needed shelf space.
### The objectives are as follows:
-	To use python/pandas to identify consumer behaviour by country
-	To use python/pandas to highlight profit rich/poor products.
-	To use python/pandas to identify customers purchasing habits 

### Online retail stocking
- Popular products – profit rich (cash cows)

- Unpopular products (loss leaders)
#### The above will be highlighted through analysing the various datasets.

### Marketing strategies
- Promotion below and above the line
- Social Media Platforms
- Pricing strategies – cost plus price, skimming, value-based pricing, competitive pricing, penetration pricing, dynamic pricing, geographic pricing, bundle pricing.

#### The above are likley to be used by various stores and those  stores that are experiencing low sales may opt for one of the above strategies.

### Limitations
